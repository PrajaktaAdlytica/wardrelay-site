BEFORE THE NEXT STEP

A few things
worth asking.

Clear expectations from the start.

Is WardRelay an operating product?+

No. This is a research-stage concept and synthetic product preview. Demand, adoption, production integrations and ROI have not been demonstrated.

What does the first pilot focus on?+

One high-variance device family in perioperative or high-value inventory. Begin by retrospectively reconstructing a shortage with purchaser-provided PO, consumption and inventory exports.

Can it automatically choose a substitute?+

No. Clinical equivalence and purchasing approval are separate, required human decisions. Missing evidence can result in a blocked or abstained case.

How does it fit with our ERP, GHX or Tecsys?+

That is a validation question. Compare the proposed workflow with the hospital’s actual licensed and configured tools. A new layer is only worth considering if it adds measurable value at acceptable effort.

What information should we bring?+

Start with your role, institution type and a description of the workflow. Do not send patient data, clinical details or records through this preview. Any later exports need bounded data rights and review.

What does the FDA shortage list tell us?+

It is a public shortage signal, not a real-time feed of each hospital’s stock or clinically safe substitutions. Local stock and candidate evidence require their own validation.

HOMEPAGE REVIEW / PRIVACY

A clear preview boundary.

This homepage’s inquiry and sign-in demonstrations process input locally in your browser. They do not transmit or store the form entries. Do not enter real credentials, patient data or sensitive records.

Architectural images depict fictional spaces. All product examples use synthetic records. The website has no product analytics, advertising trackers or live ERP connection.

Before a production inquiry service launches, WardRelay must define controller details, retention, processors, consent and a deletion/contact path. This preview notice is not a completed production privacy policy. Hosting access and logs are governed by the hosting provider.

HOMEPAGE REVIEW / TERMS

A concept for evaluation.

WardRelay is a research-stage hospital supply coordination concept. The homepage and interactive workbench demonstrate proposed design and workflow only.

Examples are synthetic and are not clinical guidance, purchasing instructions, verified inventory or approved substitute recommendations. No price acceptance, service availability, compliance status or ROI is established.

Production terms, company identity and legal review remain to be completed before an operating service is offered.

ABOUT WARDRELAY

A human-accountable
operating layer.

Our proposed mission is to start with one high-variance device-family exception board and learn whether a traceable, read-only workflow can improve the handoff from shortage signal to purchaser-approved resolution.

WardRelay is in the research and validation stage. A governed pilot depends on interviews, data mapping, a historical replay, a conditional sponsor and signed oversight approvals.
