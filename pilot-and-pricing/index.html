THE STOP RULE

A useful test can say no.

Stop or reposition if SKU/unit-of-measure reconciliation is unreliable, or the hospital’s configured GHX/Tecsys workflow already resolves the same exceptions with lower effort.

QUESTIONS, IN CONTEXT

Before the next handoff.

Is this a current subscription offer?+

No. The displayed price is a test hypothesis from the research brief, not an active commercial offer.

What does the setup hypothesis cover?+

It is a hypothesis for ERP mapping effort. Actual scope, feasibility, terms and pricing require validation with a sponsor.

Do all five modules come with the pilot?+

No. The first proposed pilot is the device-family exception board. Each additional module is a separate direction to test.

What counts as success?+

A defined change in the primary operational metric, measured against baseline, with item availability and mistaken-substitution guardrails. Clicks and demos alone do not validate demand.
