01

This website and its status

WardRelay is a research-stage concept. This notice describes the current frontend preview and the decisions still required before operating a service. It is not a finalized production privacy policy.

02

What happens to form entries

Inquiry, sign-in and recovery forms process input only in the current page to show validation and feedback. No WardRelay form endpoint receives those entries. This preview does not use cookies, local storage or analytics trackers for lead collection.

Closing or refreshing the page clears its local demonstration state. Browser autofill, password managers and hosting access logs are controlled by their respective providers.

03

Synthetic data only

The workbench contains fictional identifiers and records. It accepts no record uploads and requires no PHI. Do not enter patient information, credentials from another service or sensitive operational records.

04

Before a future inquiry service

The operator must confirm the legal controller and contact route, purposes, minimum fields, consent handling, retention, processors, security controls, rights and deletion process before collecting live inquiries.

05

Questions and deletion

No inquiry entries are retained by this frontend. The contact page demonstrates a privacy inquiry route but does not transmit it. Hosting-account or access-log questions must use the hosting provider’s available support channels.
