FIELD GUIDE / RECONSTRUCTION

Reconstruct one shortage.

An operations-learning exercise for a purchaser-provided event. Begin with workflow context; agree data rights before any records move.

ON THIS PAGEChoose a bounded eventInterview the roles separatelyAgree the evidence routeReplay the handoffMeasure and decide
01

Choose a bounded event

Start with one high-variance device family, a past shortage and a defined time window. Identify the materials executive sponsor and the operator who carried the handoff.

Record the incumbent tools, baseline source and the exact question the replay should answer.

02

Interview the roles separately

Buyer / sponsor

Where did the handoff stall? Who owns the budget? What result would justify a conditional paid pilot? Does the existing workflow already solve it?

Operator

Which records did you consult? Where did identifiers or units disagree? Who supplied missing context? What action waited for review?

Clinical / oversight owner

Who can assess equivalence? What causes abstention? Which permissions, security controls and availability expectations must be signed off?

03

Agree the evidence route

Define permitted PO, receipt, consumption and inventory extracts, plus any authorized schedule assumptions. Specify fields, source dates, owner, retention and deletion. Establish a bounded read-only route.

This website accepts no records or uploads. Do not send PHI or sensitive materials through its preview forms.

04

Replay the handoff

Align the notice, local stock, assumed demand and reviewer history. Preserve unknown mappings and conflicting sources. Compare the proposed reconstruction with the hospital’s configured incumbent process.

05

Measure and decide

Primary endpoint: median time from shortage confirmation to purchaser-approved resolution against historical events. Guardrails: item availability and mistaken substitution count.

Readiness: 8 materials/clinician interviews, 1 ERP mapping assessment, 1 shortage replay and 1 conditional paid sponsor, plus signed data-access/security and human oversight approvals.

Stop or reposition if SKU/unit-of-measure reconciliation is unreliable, or the hospital’s configured GHX/Tecsys workflow already resolves the same exceptions with lower effort.
