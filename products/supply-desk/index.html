MATERIALS MANAGERS & SUPPLY-CHAIN LEADERS

A stock number is only part of the story.

A dated ERP export, a physical count and a reserved lot can describe different quantities. The proposed board makes those distinctions visible before someone plans a transfer.

QUESTIONS, IN CONTEXT

Before the next handoff.

Does this replace an ERP?+

No. The proposed starting point is a read-only coordination layer over authorized exports. An actual data path must be assessed.

What happens when counts disagree?+

Keep both sources, their dates and the discrepancy visible. A materials reviewer resolves the conflict; the preview does not choose a true count.

What is the first pilot?+

One high-variance device-family exception board at one hospital, with a sponsor, mapped inputs and an agreed historical baseline.
