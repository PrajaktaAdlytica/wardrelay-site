SUPPLY ANALYSTS & PROCUREMENT COORDINATORS

A signal without context creates another handoff.

A notice may be old, refer to a different item or conflict with a later supplier update. Supply Proof proposes an evidence trail that keeps those questions open for a named reviewer.

QUESTIONS, IN CONTEXT

Before the next handoff.

Is monitoring connected today?+

No. These are proposed source workflows and fictional notices. Live monitoring is not an implemented capability.

Does a public shortage confirm a local gap?+

No. Local inventory, demand and item mapping require separate evidence.

How are conflicting notices handled?+

Both remain visible with their dates. The assigned source reviewer resolves the conflict or leaves it unresolved.
