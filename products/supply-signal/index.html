CLINICAL REVIEWERS, PURCHASING APPROVERS & MATERIALS COORDINATORS

A candidate is not an approval.

Availability alone cannot establish clinical equivalence. A procurement clearance cannot replace a clinical review. The proposed workflow shows both responsibilities and holds when evidence is missing.

THE PROPOSED WORKFLOW

A clear job at each step.

01

Present evidence

Record a candidate without selecting or recommending it.

02

Review separately

Clinical and purchasing roles inspect their own evidence.

03

Hold or hand off

Keep missing decisions blocked and preserve attributed review history.

QUESTIONS, IN CONTEXT

Before the next handoff.

Does recording a demo review approve a substitute?+

No. The controls only record that a fictional review occurred. A decision remains required, and no candidate is selected.

What if a clinical review is missing?+

The handoff remains blocked. Purchasing activity cannot substitute for clinical-equivalence review.

Can decisions be corrected?+

The proposed audit model retains actor, time and reason. This isolated preview can be reset without affecting any external system.
